Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 18, 2026

MyBudgetApp respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our personal finance management application.

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account or sign in, we may process the following information:

  • Name and email address.
  • Authentication information required to verify your account.
  • Basic account information provided through Google Sign-In, if you choose that option.

2. Financial Information and Cloud Sync

Your income, expenses, budgets, savings, shop debt records, welfare records and other financial entries are stored locally on your device and securely synchronized with Firebase Database to maintain your records across sessions.

We take reasonable measures to protect your financial data during transmission and storage, but you should also protect your device with an appropriate screen lock.

3. Firebase Authentication and Database

MyBudgetApp uses Google Firebase Authentication and Firebase Database to manage account registration, sign-in, and data synchronization. Relevant account and financial record information is processed and stored securely through Google Firebase services.

Google's privacy information is available at Google Privacy Policy.

4. How We Use Information

Authentication and financial information are used to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Verify user identity during sign-in.
  • Synchronize and maintain your financial records securely via Firebase.
  • Support account-related requests.

5. Information Sharing

We do not sell or rent users' personal or financial information. Information is processed and stored by Firebase and Google as necessary to provide authentication and database synchronization services.

We may disclose information when required by applicable law or a valid legal request.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Financial records remain stored in your account and local device storage until they are deleted by you or your account is removed.

You can request the deletion of your account and associated database records by contacting us using the email below.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect account and financial information through secure Firebase services and encryption standards. However, no digital storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You are responsible for keeping your device and account credentials secure.

8. Children's Privacy

MyBudgetApp is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children below the minimum age required by applicable law to use the relevant services independently. Where required, parental or guardian consent must be obtained.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when application features or data practices change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
