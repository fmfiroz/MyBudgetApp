Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 18, 2026

MyBudgetApp respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our personal finance management application.

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account or sign in, we may process the following information:

  • Name and email address.
  • Authentication information required to verify your account.
  • Basic account information provided through Google Sign-In, if you choose that option.

2. Financial Information and Local Storage

Your income, expenses, budgets, savings, shop debt records, welfare records and other financial entries are designed to be stored locally on your own device.

These financial records are not intentionally uploaded to or stored on MyBudgetApp's Firebase servers under the current design.

Anyone with access to your unlocked device or its app data may potentially access locally stored information. Please protect your device with an appropriate screen lock.

3. Firebase Authentication

MyBudgetApp uses Google Firebase Authentication to manage account registration and sign-in. If you use email/password or Google Sign-In, relevant account and authentication information is processed by Google and Firebase.

Google's privacy information is available at Google Privacy Policy.

4. How We Use Information

Authentication information is used to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Verify user identity during sign-in.
  • Maintain account authentication sessions.
  • Support account-related requests.

5. Information Sharing

We do not sell or rent users' personal information. Authentication information is processed by Firebase and Google as necessary to provide sign-in services.

We may disclose information when required by applicable law or a valid legal request.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Financial records remain on your device until they are deleted by you or removed through device or app-data management.

Uninstalling the application or clearing its data may remove locally stored financial records. These records may not be recoverable.

Your Firebase account is separate from your locally stored financial records. To request deletion of your account, contact us using the email below.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect account information through the authentication services we use. However, no digital storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You are responsible for keeping your device and account credentials secure.

8. Children's Privacy

MyBudgetApp is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children below the minimum age required by applicable law to use the relevant services independently. Where required, parental or guardian consent must be obtained.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when application features or data practices change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
